## 👋 About us

We're a couple who build games for the joy of it. Each project explores a
different style of play, but they all share the same goal: a pleasant way
to spend your time.

## 🎮 Live games

### [Tostik Town](https://yilmazgamestudio.github.io/tostik-town/)

A top-down toastie shop game for the browser. Press every toastie by hand,
serve the neighborhood and grow your tiny shop.

**[▶ Play now](https://yilmazgamestudio.github.io/tostik-town/)**

## 🃏 Serving the game

### [King Skor Takip](https://kingskortakip.com.tr/)

A free score tracker for the card game King. Enter your four players, choose
a game mode and keep penalties, trumps, hands and points in one place, so you
can focus on the table instead of the math.
